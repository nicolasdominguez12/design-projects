# HedgePilotX — Portfolio and Résumé Summary

**Category:** Quantitative Finance · Options Greeks · Risk Management · Feedback Control

**Research objective:** Compare dynamic hedging strategies under simulated changing markets, trading frictions, and explicit risk budgets.

**Prototype:** Designed a research dashboard for evaluating hedge-controller efficiency, inspecting path-by-path trades and risk, stress-testing market regimes, separating controller selection from a held-out evaluation, and storing/contrasting research runs.

**Cross-disciplinary connection:** Mechanical-engineering control concepts—feedback, constrained optimization, dynamic systems, and robustness—applied to derivatives risk management.

## Suggested résumé bullet

- Built HedgePilotX, an options-hedging research prototype comparing adaptive rebalancing strategies across simulated risk–cost frontiers, pathwise hedge replay, stress scenarios, and held-out validation.

The demonstrated results are simulated. No independently verified outperformance, live trading performance, or public reproducible code is claimed.
