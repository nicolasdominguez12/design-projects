# MatSelect AI — Portfolio and Résumé Summary

**Category:** Mechanical Engineering · AI-Assisted Material Selection · Multi-Criteria Decision Support

**Problem:** Engineers must balance material performance, manufacturability, environment, cost, and availability rather than optimize just one property.

**Prototype:** Built a web interface that gathers design requirements and produces a ranked shortlist with criteria-based comparisons, radar visualization, selection rationale, and stated limitations. The recorded demo includes DP600 dual-phase steel and AA6016-T4 aluminum among example candidates.

**Engineering emphasis:** Explainable trade-offs and constraints; final recommendations still require verified property data, component-specific analysis, and engineering review.

## Suggested résumé bullet

- Developed MatSelect AI, a material-selection web prototype that translates design requirements into ranked engineering options with comparative scoring, multi-criteria visualization, and interpretable trade-offs.

## Verification status

Public case-study documentation only. Source, underlying data, scoring implementation, and accuracy testing are not released in this repository. Do not claim validated engineering performance or measured time savings without evidence.
